Cutter Flower

Symphyotrichum ericoides(L.) G.L.Nesom

A delicate cluster of pale blossoms oft dismissed as mere adornment beside grander blooms. Favoured by florists for its softness and quiet volume, the Cutter Bloom is said to linger longest in arrangements prepared for unspoken affections and unfinished farewells. Though harmless in appearance, prolonged exposure to its dried pollen is rumoured to deepen sentimentality and disturb restful sleep in susceptible hearts.

Damask Rose

Rosa damascena Mill.

A richly layered rose long cultivated within secluded monastery gardens and noble estates alike. Revered for its deep fragrance and velvety petals, the Damask Rose is frequently prepared for declarations too earnest to remain concealed. Distilled oil from mature blooms is prized among perfumers and apothecaries, said to soothe grief whilst sharpening longing in equal measure.

Camellia

Camellia japonica L.

An immaculate bloom of soft structure and near-perfect symmetry, favoured in arrangements where restraint is valued above extravagance. Though absent of strong fragrance, the Camellia is admired for its enduring freshness once cut. Florists oft associate it with dignified affection, quiet loyalty, and sentiments carried without expectation of return.

7 DNS per stem

Hydrangea

Hydrangea macrophylla (Thunb.) Ser.

A voluminous cluster bloom known to alter subtly in hue according to the soil in which it is raised. Coveted for its cloud-like fullness, the Hydrangea is often chosen for arrangements concerning apology, remembrance, and emotions too complicated for simpler flowers to bear. Improper drying may result in bitterness of scent.

9 DNS per bloom

Pompon

Chrysanthemum morifolium

A meticulously cultivated chrysanthemum bearing compact spherical blooms of remarkable uniformity. Though outwardly cheerful in appearance, it remaineth closely tied to rituals of mourning and ancestral devotion throughout several eastern provinces. Its petals retain form long after drying, making it suitable for keepsakes and memorial preservation.

5 DNS per stem

Carnation

Dianthus caryophyllus L.

A hardy flowering stem distinguished by ruffled petals and an unexpectedly clove-like fragrance. Long associated with enduring affection amongst common folk, the Carnation is neither rare nor extravagant, yet persisteth where finer blooms oft wither. Certain crimson cultivars are quietly exchanged in districts where open courtship remaineth discouraged.

4 DNS per stem

The Florist

Caoimhe Winchester

At present, Florencia remaineth under the keeping of Miss Caoimhe Winchester, by whose hand the greater part of all arrangements are fashioned. Thus, it stirreth little surprise should the shop door remain fast shut for the better course of a day, whilst letters of reply continueth nonetheless to depart with unfailing punctuality — most especially those bearing the seals of ancient houses from the inner quarter.

ㅤ

The establishment hath grown known for methods of preservation deemed uncommon, for blooms seldom espied within the common markets, and for certain distilled preparations whose cultivation records appear nowhere plainly kept. Some patrons have returned unto Florencia for years together, yet remain wholly ignorant as to whence particular flowers within the shop are truly gathered.

ㅤ

Few, if any, have witnessed Miss Winchester purchasing stock from market merchants or public growers. Nevertheless, fresh blooms persist within the shop in every season alike, including several varieties long believed lost beyond these lands. Many patrons depart Florencia with an unease difficult to name, as though they had confessed somewhat more than first intended. There are, besides, quieter rumours oft exchanged in lowered voice — that certain flowers sold by Miss Winchester refuseth unfamiliar hands altogether, and that some arrangements are never displayed openly within the shop, nor spoken of except through private correspondence.


Walk-ins be welcome. Pray keep thy hands from the shelves beyond.

The Bloom Ledger

All transactions are recorded in a proprietary notation system, written in elemental ink — visible only under sustained fire-weave light. The ledger is stored beneath the third floorboard from the east wall, behind the pressing cabinet.

Entries include client pseudonyms, extract type, volume, and delivery method. No real names. The florist does not need them. She remembers faces.

Notes on Certain Rootwork

Certain extracts cannot be obtained through conventional botanical practice alone. Preparations marked as elemental-assisted require conditions not reproducible by ordinary cultivation or laboratory methods. The proprietress does not disclose her process. Availability remains dependent upon the condition and disposition of the source material at time of harvest. Some commissions may therefore be declined without further explanation. The deposit is non-refundable.

Moonwake — Exposure Risk

A long-enduring patron hath purchas’d the preparation upon seven several occasions these two years past. Lately, a second intermediary hath arriv’d with questions that mirror the first. Further discourse is kept to a whisper, until intent be made plain.

Independent mage-investigators do trace compounds that answer not unto any known record of cultivation. They abide patient, methodical, and ever harder to turn aside from their course.

Winter Wisteria — Shortage

Frost-wound hath cut this season’s bloom-count by near the half. Several standing patrons are like to receive not their wonted share.

Rosethorn Rite — Improper Use

Reports do speak of a diluted counterfeit abroad amongst the noble districts. One receiver fell into an obsessive attachment that linger’d beyond the term appointed.

Rue Pallid — Contamination

Two late batches did show strange bitterness, with an excess of sharpen’d wit and thought. The stock is isolated, pending assay beneath fire-weave light.

Mourning Jasmine — Demand Increase

The funeral districts continue to ask quantities beyond the bounds of sustainable gathering. More must needs be sought beyond the eastern marsh-routes.

Belladonna Hymnal — Restrictions

The ecclesiastical authorities have renew’d their inquiry concerning compounds touch’d by auditory visions and devotional unrest. Open carriage is no longer advis’d.

Evermere — Weariness Mask

Repeated requests now do exceed the season’s store. Diverse patrons ask for larger measures than erstwhile, seeking naught save the brief concealment it doth bestow.

Hushwater — Memory Drift

Small discordances have appear’d in recent accounts after private tastings. Notes taken within the hour no longer match the selfsame events by nightfall.

Laurel Sanguine — Attachment Residue

A few receivers have reported a fixation most persistent, even after the effect hath pass’d. The matter is handled softly, lest notice be drawn.

Flos Viviea — Unsafe Demand

Interest hath increas’d among those that seek swift vitality despite the known perils. Distribution is held beneath stricter lock and seal.

Lascivia — Appetite Quiet

Requests for the bloom have risen among patrons that seek restraint rather than indulgence. Several houses now keep it seal’d for private use, praising its steadier governance of bodily urge.
